What to change in the HTML

  • Replace the words between <h3> and </h3> with the person’s name.
  • Replace the paragraph text between <p> and </p>. Keep the opening and closing tags.
  • For a photo, use the form above, or replace the initials block with an <img>. Its src is the image address and alt is its description.
  • Change the email in both places: after mailto: and in the visible link text.

Why this layout works

display: flex puts the image and text next to each other. flex-wrap: wrap lets them stack when space runs out. gap: 16px sets the space between them.

To add another person, paste a second copy of the whole snippet. Keep the heading order: the page’s section heading should be <h2>, followed by each person’s <h3>.

Set it up, step by step

  1. Choose your video in Panopto. Check that your students have permission to view it.
  2. In Share → Embed, choose Interactivity: All and enable captions if available.
  3. Paste the embed address into the field above and select Load my video. This fills in both the embed and the “Open in Panopto” link.
  4. Copy the HTML into Canvas. Test the contents menu, captions and full viewer link in Student View.

Editing by hand? The iframe’s src is the embed address. The link’s href is the full viewer address. Replace the host and video ID in both addresses with your own. Keep Embed.aspx for the iframe and Viewer.aspx for the link.

Keep the contents accessible

interactivity=all enables the viewer’s interactive features. The recording must already have contents entries; this HTML does not create chapters or captions.

width: 100% fills the available space. height: 620px leaves room for the viewer and its menus. Change the height if your video needs more space.

Always offer another way in

The snippet includes an “Open in Panopto” link. On a small screen, or if the embed cannot load, students can use the full viewer. Check captions for accuracy and provide a transcript when needed.

Your snippetHTML · ready for Canvas

What to change in the HTML

  • <details> holds one expandable section. Its <summary> is the heading students click.
  • Replace the words inside <summary> and the paragraph text inside <p>. Keep the tags around them.
  • A sub-accordion is another complete <details>…</details> block inside the outer section’s content. Add as many as you need, or remove every inner block for a simple accordion.
  • The generator treats your text as plain text. Leave a blank line to start another paragraph. After pasting into Canvas, use the visual editor to add links, images or lists.

Keep the structure clear

Use short, descriptive headings. Keep the most important instructions visible on the page, and use accordions for supporting information. Too many levels can make content hard to find; this builder uses one level of sub-accordions.

The open attribute starts a section expanded. Remove the attribute to start collapsed: open="false" still opens it.

University defaults, with optional changes

The snippet retains fossa fossa-r. By default, the main heading uses 0.8em padding; main content and sub-accordions use 0.5em. There are no added borders, margins, sub-heading padding or inner content wrappers. Text colour inherits from the surrounding page. Extra styling from fossa depends on your University’s CSS.
